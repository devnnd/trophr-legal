Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Devendra Mehta, the individual developer operating trophr (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the app and these legal pages. Privacy contact: devmoonbound@gmail.com.

1. Scope

This policy describes the current trophr preview’s account, onboarding, subscription, local storage, synchronization, and optional test-export features. It does not claim that planned workout logging, cloud photo uploads, private clubs, challenges, or other future features are already available. We will update this policy before introducing materially different data collection or sharing.

3. Fitness information and device permissions

Your training choices and any fitness-related information you voluntarily provide can be sensitive. The current preview does not read Apple Health or HealthKit data, scan your body, make medical diagnoses, or request your contacts or precise location. It does not use your photos for facial recognition or advertising.

The optional photo test uses Apple’s picker so you can choose specific images. Selecting or sharing a photo is optional. You can manage available permissions in iOS Settings. Information displayed in a rest Live Activity may be visible on your Lock Screen to someone with access to your device.

4. How and with whom information is shared

We use service providers for specific functions:

  • Supabase: account authentication, hosting, database storage, and supported account synchronization. Account and synced records are processed through this service. See Supabase’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: subscription verification, purchase restoration, and subscription status. When you link membership to your trophr account, its account identifier is associated with your RevenueCat customer record. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, and iOS services you use. Apple also processes information under its own privacy policy.
  • Email services: services delivering requested sign-in messages and handling support correspondence process the email address and message content needed for those purposes.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts these public legal pages. Visiting them involves ordinary web requests, including your IP address and browser information, processed by GitHub. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Providers may use their own infrastructure and subprocessors. We also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, prevent abuse, or protect people’s rights and security. If the service changes ownership, any transfer of personal information must remain subject to applicable law and appropriate notice.

If you choose to share a recovery file or media export, your selected recipient or service receives that file and handles it under its own practices. A recovery export can contain private account records and unsynced changes. Our deletion of a local temporary file does not delete copies you have saved or sent elsewhere.

5. Advertising and analytics

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking in this preview. We have not added a separate product-analytics or session-replay SDK. This does not mean no operational information is processed: authentication, hosting, and subscription providers process information necessary for their services, including subscription reporting.

These legal pages do not include our own analytics scripts, advertising pixels, external fonts, or cookie-based tracking. GitHub’s hosting practices are described in its linked statement.

6. Local storage and security

The app stores setup and supported records in local databases. Authentication tokens are stored using iOS secure storage. Account requests use HTTPS, and backend access controls are designed to restrict private records to the relevant account and authorized service operations. We do not claim that all local records are separately encrypted by trophr or that the service is end-to-end encrypted.

No system is completely secure. Protect access to your device and sign-in account. Device backups, operating-system features, and files you export are also subject to your device and destination settings.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep account and synced information while it is needed to provide your account and the features you use. We also retain limited records where needed to handle billing, security, disputes, or legal obligations. Retention depends on the record’s purpose, whether your account remains active, applicable legal duties, and service-provider backup and logging arrangements.

You may request account deletion by emailing devmoonbound@gmail.com. Use your account email where possible; if you use Apple’s private relay, identify that relay address. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify ownership, but will not ask for your password or sign-in code. We will explain any information we must retain and respond within the period required by applicable law.

The current preview does not yet provide in-app deletion of your server account. Signing out and confirming removal of device data removes that account’s local database and local recovery cache; it does not delete the server account, all app setup, copies you exported, or your subscription. Do not remove unsynced data before preserving anything you need.

Deletion from active systems does not necessarily immediately remove protected backup copies or records that Apple or another provider is independently required to keep. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage it separately in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

8. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to create an account, share optional content, or grant optional permissions, although some features require the relevant information. Available account controls let you update supported profile information and export a local recovery copy.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact devmoonbound@gmail.com to make a request. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. We do not penalize you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

9. Legal grounds and international processing

Where a legal basis is required, we process account and service information to provide the service you request; use consent where required for optional or sensitive processing; and rely on applicable legal obligations or legitimate interests for necessary security, support, and fraud prevention, subject to your rights. This policy is not a substitute for any separate consent required by law.

Our providers operate internationally, so information may be processed outside your home country. The location of one hosting database does not determine every provider’s processing location. International processing is subject to applicable data-protection requirements. Contact us for information about the processing arrangements relevant to your account.

10. Adults only

trophr is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through trophr, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. Changes and contact

We will revise this policy as our practices change, update the date above, and provide additional notice or request consent where required for material changes. New collection or sharing is not authorized merely because a feature appears in our development plans.
